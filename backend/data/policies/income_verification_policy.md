# Income Verification Policy

## 1. Overview and Scope
This policy outlines the standard operating procedures for verifying applicant income across submitted financial documents including payslips, bank statements, and income tax returns (ITR).

## 2. Salary and Income Discrepancies
- **Acceptable Variation**: An annual income variation of up to 10% between declared income, payslip projection (monthly net x 12), and tax return declared income is considered acceptable due to variable bonuses, tax deductions, or seasonal overtime.
- **Medium Risk Discrepancy (10% - 20%)**: Income mismatches between 10% and 20% must be flagged for underwriting review. The loan officer must check bank statement salary credit amounts to verify actual cash flow.
- **High Risk Discrepancy (> 20%)**: Income discrepancies exceeding 20% across documents indicate potential document alteration or misrepresentation. High risk flags require explicit human underwriter sign-off and secondary document verification.

## 3. Required Document Proofs
- Salaried applicants must provide at least 3 consecutive months of payslips and 6 months of bank account statements.
- Self-employed applicants must provide 2 consecutive years of audited Income Tax Returns along with profit & loss statements.
